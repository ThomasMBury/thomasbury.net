Bury Group, UC Riverside

Welcome to the research group of  Thomas Bury, Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at UC Riverside. We develop mathematical and computational tools to understand complex systems, with a focus on tipping points and early warning signals. Our work combines nonlinear dynamics, machine learning, and data science, with applications to cardiology, ecology, and climate science.
