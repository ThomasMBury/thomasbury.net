    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Early Warning Signals</h3>
    <p style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6;">We develop theory and computational tools — including deep learning classifiers — to detect approaching tipping points in dynamical systems before they occur.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 20px; border: 1px solid #e0e0e0; border-radius: 8px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Cardiac Arrhythmia</h3>
    <p style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6;">We use mathematical models and machine learning to understand and predict arrhythmia dynamics from wearable ECG data, in collaboration with cardiologists at UBC and Weill Cornell.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 20px; border: 1px solid #e0e0e0; border-radius: 8px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Spiral Waves in Excitable Media</h3>
    <p style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6;">We study transitions to spiral wave dynamics — responsible for ventricular tachycardia — using reinforcement learning and experiments in cardiac tissue culture.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="padding: 20px; border: 1px solid #e0e0e0; border-radius: 8px;">
    <h3 style="margin-top: 0;">Mechanistic Biological Modelling</h3>
    <p style="font-size: 14px; line-height: 1.6;">We build and analyse mechanistic models of ecological, epidemiological, and Earth system dynamics, with a focus on how human behaviour couples to these systems.</p>
  </div>
