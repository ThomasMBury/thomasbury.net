We are excited to officially launch the Bury Research Group in the [Department of Mathematics](https://mathdept.ucr.edu/) at the University of California, Riverside.

Our group works at the intersection of nonlinear dynamics, data science, and machine learning, with a focus on tipping points and early warning signals in complex systems. Applications span cardiology, ecology, and climate science.

We are actively recruiting motivated PhD students and postdocs — see the [Contact](/contact/) page if you are interested in joining.
